Performance-guided

Adaptive Practice

Builds a fresh 10-question round from your own saved Pharm-let results: what you have missed, what you have not practiced much, and what you mastered long enough ago to be worth revisiting. Each round is chosen when you start it, so finishing one changes what the next one asks. This is separate from Week Practice above, which is unchanged.

Scope is cumulative and never runs ahead of the week you pick: choosing Week 6 draws on Weeks 1–6 only. With no saved results yet, the round is simply balanced practice across that range.
